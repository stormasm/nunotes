The Nushell team is also saddened by the passing of Yash.

We found out about his passing recently and wanted to acknowledge his dedication and contributions to our project and also the friendship he shared among the nushell community. Our condolences to his friends and family members.

Yash started working on Nushell at the young age of 19 in September of 2023.

He quickly learned and came up to speed on the project and in short time was a valuable contributor to the inner workings of Reedline, the parser, completions and other components. Upon his passing he was also helping to develop a new parser for Nushell.

Please consider reading the beautiful obituary that was written for Yash to learn more about his life, passions, interests and overall amazing spirit !
