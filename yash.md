The Nushell team is also saddened by the passing of Yash.

We found out about his passing recently and wanted to acknowledge
his dedication to not only our project but the friendship he
shared among our community of members.  

Yash started working on Nushell at the young age of 19 in September of 2023.

He quickly learned and came up to speed on the project and in short time was
a valuable contributor to the inner workings of Reedline, the parser, completions
and other components.  Upon his passing he was also helping to develop a
new parser for Nushell.

We would like to acknowledge Yash and his contributions over the past number
of years along with our condolences to his friends and family members.

Please consider reading the beautiful obituary that was written for Yash to
learn more about his life, passions, interests and overall amazing spirit !
